### Prelude
Since the mid-17th century, Dutch settlers had established the **Cape Colony** settlement in what is now South Africa. They called themselves Afrikaners and spoke their own dialect of **Afrikaans**, but are more often referred to as **Boers**, meaning "peasant" in Dutch. They seized lands from and enslaved the natives of South Africa. After the Battle of Cape Town in 1806 during the Napoleonic Wars, Great Britain maintained permanent control over the colony after 1815, but conflicts over language, culture, and legislation arose between the Boers and the British, which came to a head in 1834 when Britain outlawed slavery.

This prompted a mass exodus of Boers known as the , who relied on slaves to run their farms, out of the colony to found new independent settlements in the east, an event known as the **Great Trek (1835-1840)**. There they founded two new republics: the **Orange Free State** and the **South African Republic (Transvaal)**, where they continued the practice of slavery and stole land from local Africans.

![[Pasted image 20260531002554.png]]
As Britain expanded its territory, it came to share borders with the Boer republics, with border skirmishes eventually leading to the **First Boer War (1880-1881)**. The Boers were victorious, and the British were forced to acknowledge the Boer republics as independent states.
