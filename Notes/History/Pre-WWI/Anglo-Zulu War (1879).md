### The Zulu Kingdom
![[Pasted image 20260606233926.png]]
In the 1870s, three main powers compete for territory in Southern Africa: Great Britain, the Boer Republics, and the Zulu Kingdom. The Zulu Kingdom came to be when King **Shaka Zulu** spearheaded a revolution in 1816, uniting the various clans under the **AmaZulu** (or Zulu). 

![[Pasted image 20260606234712.png]]
As ruler of the new kingdom, Shaka introduced many reforms in the military. He introduced conscription for all able-bodied men, with training starting at the age of 14. The **impis** (warriors) are equipped with short stabbing **Assegai** (spears) called **iKlwa**, named after the sound it makes when ripped from an enemy's flesh. Along with an assegai, warriors wielded a cowhide shield (**isiHlangu**). Other weapons include the **isiJula** (throwing spear) and the **iWisa** (club).

![[Pasted image 20260606234842.png]]
Additionally, Shaka introduces a successful battle formation called **izimpondo zankomo** (the horns of the buffalo), which is comprised of four sections of soldiers: the chest to engage the enemy and hold them in place, the two horns to outflank and encircle the enemy, and the loins serving as the reserve. With these military advancements, Shaka transforms tribal warfare from what was merely a ritual show of force into campaigns of crushing annihilation and annexation. Through this, conquered tribes are incorporated into the kingdom, and his army grows from 350 to about 20,000 men in just a few years, with his kingdom as large as 30,000 square km. Though Shaka is assassinated by his two half-brothers in 1828, his military empire lives on.
### European Arrival
In 1824, the British establish what eventually becomes the trading hub of **Durban**. Tensions rise between the Zulu and the British as the latter seek to expand their territory. In the 1830s, the Boers/Voortrekkers, who had been living in Africa since the 17th century, were now migrating westward to establish new republics as tensions between them and the British increase. These new Boer territories conflict with Zulu territory, which eventually leads to border clashes
### Battle of Blood River
![[Pasted image 20260607001014.png]]
In 1838, during the Boers' **Great Trek**, the Boers and the Zulu agreed to a peaceable land treaty, however this was broken by the Zulu King, believing he had the strength in numbers to overpower the Boers. During the **Piet Retief Delegation massacre** in February 1838, after the treaty between the two peoples had just been negotiated, the Zulus massacred 100 Boers of the delegation party, starting a wider war between the Boers, British, and the Zulus, who were undergoing an internal power struggle at the time. The Zulu warriors sack the porty city of Durban, but are dealt a devastating defeat at **Blood River**, with over 3,000 Zulu casualties to just three lost by the Boers. In the aftermath of the war, the Zulus are forced to cede land to the Boers and the British.

Countless skirmishes between the three groups occur in the decades afterward.
