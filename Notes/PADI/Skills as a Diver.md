### Deep Water Entry
#### Giant Stride
A very common entry from charter dive boats, docks, and other stable platforms up to a couple metres above the water.
- enter the water fully equipped with the BCD partially inflated.
- hold onto the regulator and mask as you enter, breathing through it.
- look straight ahead and take a big step in, don't jump.
- signal "okay" and clear the entry area, then switch to your snorkel.
### Weight Check
- check your weight in the water with a buddy, making adjustments one at a time.
- you should float at eye level with an empty BCD and holding a normal breath; you should slowly sink when you exhale.
- after this, add two kg/5 lbs if you checked with a full cylinder. This is because the air in the tank has weight, and as it is used up, you will become lighter. The added weight will offset this weight change.
### Dealing with a Loose Cylinder Band
Sometimes the band holding the cylinder in the BCD can become loose.
- if it loosens while exiting, ease back into the water, slip out of the kit and then lift it out of the water by the cylinder valve.
- if it comes loose before the dive, help the diver/get help to remove the scuba kit to retighten it
- if it comes loose during a dive, the cylinder tends to stay with the diver. Signal the affected diver/signal your buddy so you can have it tightened.
- when tightening the band, have the diver face downward, loosen the band so that the cylinder can be adjusted, then make the adjustment and retighten it, and then lock it.
### Snorkel Clearing
Clearing the snorkel is a routine skill used at the surface, since the snorkel fills with water whenever it is out of your mouth.
- blow the water out forcefully (the **blast method**).
- be cautious of leftover water when taking your first breath. Use airway control to breathe past any water that remains. Blast out any residual water if necessary.
### Neutral Buoyancy
One popular technique for achieving neutral buoyancy is the **fin pivot**. 
- lie face down on the bottom
- when neutrally buoyant, you should pivot upward on your fin tips when you inhale and downward when you exhale.
- add or release air from you BCD in small amounts when adjusting buoyancy.
