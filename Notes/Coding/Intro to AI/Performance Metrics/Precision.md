**Precision**, also known as the **positive predictive value (PPV)**, measures the proportion of predictions were correct. In other words, out of all positive predictions made, how many were actually true positives?
$$Precision = \frac{TP}{TP+FP}$$

Precision can be misleading; an algorithm that makes only a small amount of high-confidence predictions may have a high precision score, but this may not necessarily extrapolate to a greater amount of predictions.
