Child proof caps save lives every year by preventing children from accessing harmful drugs and substances.

There are four popular mechanisms for child-proof caps.
#### Align the Triangles
![[Pasted image 20260527105315.png|156]]![[Pasted image 20260527105437.png|253]]![[Pasted image 20260527105534.png|282]]
There are two triangles; one on the cap, and one on the bottle. These must be lined up before the bottle is opened. At the opening of the bottle is a ridge that runs the entire circumference, except for an opening at the triangle. Under the cap are three notches on the underside, with one smaller than the others located at the triangle. When the lid is on, these notches are held down by the ridge, preventing the lid from opening. When the triangles are aligned, the small ridge is able to pass through the gap in the ridge, allowing the cap to slide off the bottle.

#### Push the Tab Down
![[Pasted image 20260528193139.png|258]]![[Pasted image 20260528194049.png|340]]
To open this bottle, push down on the tab and twist the cap off. Near the opening is a small sloped piece sticking out. When the tab is pushed down, this piece is pulled down.

![[Pasted image 20260528194236.png]]
There are two notches on the inside of the cap. The piece is sloped in such a way that allows the notches to slide over in one direction, but not the other when trying to open the cap. Only when the tab is pushed down are the notches free to slide over and allow the cap to lift up.
#### Squeeze the Sides
![[Pasted image 20260528194448.png|296]]![[Pasted image 20260528194718.png|353]]
This cap does not move unless you squeeze on the smooth surface on both sides. Near the opening on the bottle are two sloped notches. There are also two sloped notches on the inside of the cap located 90 degrees from each smooth face. 

![[Pasted image 20260528194825.png]]
When you squeeze the sides of the cap, the notches on the cap are pushed outward, avoiding the sloped notches and allowing the cap to be removed.
#### Push the Cap Down
![[Pasted image 20260528195100.png|164]]![[Pasted image 20260528195038.png|502]]
This cap can freely turn but won't come off unless you push down on it. The cap has a separate component inside it with grooves that run all the way around.

![[Pasted image 20260528195235.png|216]]![[Pasted image 20260528195432.png|224]]![[Pasted image 20260528195347.png|238]]
Underneath the cap are notches all the way around. Normally, the inner component does not move and keeps the cap secured to the bottle. When the lid is pushed down, the notches interlock with the grooves, allowing the cap and the inner layer to move in sync, and thus the cap can be removed after twisting.
