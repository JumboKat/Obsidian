#History #Greece 
### Prelude
After uniting most of Greece (Sparta excluded) under the **Hellenic League**, or **League of Corinth**, King **Philip II** of Macedonia planned a **Panhellenic** (all-Greek) war against the Achaemenid (Persian) Empire, who was now weak and disorganized. However, in 336 BC, just before his great campaign, he was assassinated by the captain of his bodyguards. Succeeding him would be his 20 year-old son Alexander.
