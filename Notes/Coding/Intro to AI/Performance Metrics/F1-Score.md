**F<sub>1</sub>** is the **harmonic mean** of [[Precision||precision]] and [[Recall||recall]]. It is calculated as:
$$F_1score=2\times\frac{precision\times recall}{precision+recall}$$
A high F1 score requires both precision and recall to be high. Both are given equal importance, and so F1 score may not be fit for applications with unequal error costs.

It is often a trade-off, since increasing recall can decrease precision.
