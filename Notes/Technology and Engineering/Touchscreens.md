The touchscreen display is a revolutionary piece of technology that paved the way for the ubiquitous modern smartphone. It is the product of a seamless integration of three different technologies: toughened glass, a capacitive touchscreen, and an OLED display.

The first computer with a touchscreen was the HP-150, commercialized in 1983 by the HP Company.
